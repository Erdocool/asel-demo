Hikâyemiz

We started Asel Pneumatic Tools as a brand where quality, power, and performance come together. With our passion and expertise in pneumatic tools used in a wide range of applications including industry, construction, automotive, manufacturing, and hobby projects, we are determined to become a trusted name in the sector.

We aim to bring together durable, efficient, and long-lasting products by understanding our customers' needs in the best way possible. The pneumatic tools in our product portfolio are selected considering high performance, ease of use, and reliability criteria, helping users do their work faster, safer, and more efficiently.

Kullanma kılavuzlarında KILIÇ GRUP A.Ş. anılır. Üretim: Made in Türkiye.

Değerlerimiz

Quality and Reliability Each pneumatic tool is meticulously designed and manufactured in accordance with our high standards. Sağlam malzemeler, dayanıklı yapılar ve mükemmel işçilik.
